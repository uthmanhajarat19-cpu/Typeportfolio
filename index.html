Simulation · Zoho Desk

1. Leaked item complaint

A customer’s body oil arrived leaking and he needed it sorted before Friday.

Problem

Order #2087 arrived with a leaking bottle. The customer wanted a replacement or refund because he was travelling on Friday.

Action

  • Apologised, then asked for a photo and the transaction reference
  • Set a clear expectation: escalation to the team lead with an update in 30 minutes
  • Wrote a private note with the order, evidence and approval, and asked the delivery team to review packing
  • Confirmed a delivery time that suited the customer

Outcome

The manager approved a replacement. Delivery was agreed for 12 pm the next day and the customer confirmed he would be available.

Simulation · Zendesk

2. Product recommendation and payment check

A customer needed help choosing a perfume, then needed her payment confirmed before delivery.

Problem

She wanted a gift for her sister but was unsure what to pick. After choosing, she sent a payment receipt and needed the order confirmed.

Action

  • Asked what scents her sister likes before recommending anything
  • Offered two options with prices and when each suits, then asked for her delivery date
  • Asked for the payment receipt and delivery details
  • Logged an internal note to verify payment before dispatch, then confirmed the payment and delivery time

Outcome

Payment was verified and delivery was confirmed for Saturday by 4 pm.

Simulation · Zendesk

3. Rescheduling an appointment and verifying identity

A patient wanted to move her appointment and was waiting on a lab result.

Problem

She was travelling for work, needed to move her appointment to Friday, and had not received last week’s lab result.

Action

  • Offered the available Friday slots so she could choose
  • Asked for her full name and date of birth to verify identity before discussing the result
  • Checked with the lab and gave a clear collection time
  • Moved the appointment and logged an internal note

Outcome

The appointment was moved to 9 am on Friday, identity was verified, and the result was available for collection from 4 pm.
